Two-factor check, and what we keep

To keep out spam and fake programs, posting needs two factors: a one-time code we email you, and a code from an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, 1Password and similar), which you set up once on your first suggestion.

We do not keep your email address. We keep an unreadable fingerprint of it (a keyed hash), your authenticator key, the time and your suggestion. No IP address. Ask hello@lucaintini.ai to delete a suggestion at any time. The funding finder itself still collects nothing.
